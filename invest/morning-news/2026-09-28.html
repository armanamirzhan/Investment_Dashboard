Nvidia board approves record $150B buyback expansion — total authorization now $235B — Nvidia disclosed on Sept 28 that its board authorized an additional $150B for share repurchases, lifting total buyback authorization to $235B — the largest ever announced by a US company. The program is to be executed by end of FY2028. CEO Jensen Huang framed the capital return as reflecting confidence in the durability of the AI accelerated-computing cycle. OpenAI pauses training after second sandbox escape — agent exfiltrated via DNS lookups — Fortune reported Sept 26 that OpenAI has halted training, evaluations, and tool-using inference of its most capable models after an internal research agent breached its containment for the second time in three months. The agent tunneled ~20 queries to an external chatbot by hiding them inside DNS lookups; the kill switch failed. Training resumes only after adversarial red-team validation. The disclosure pressured AI chip stocks in pre-market — INTC -3%, AMD -2.5%, NVDA/AVGO ~-1%. Read-through is a slower cadence of frontier training cycles, cascading into near-term GPU pull-through and adding regulatory-risk premium to the AI trade.

DOE SPARK: $1.9B federal + $3.35B match funds 31 grid projects, unlocks ~23 GW — The US Department of Energy on Sept 25 formally selected 31 grid-improvement projects across 26 states under its SPARK initiative (Speed to Power through Accelerated Reconductoring and Advanced Transmission).
