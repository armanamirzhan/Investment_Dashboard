Commerce testimony to Congress (July 14): H200 shipments to China 'very few' despite ~$10B in approved BIS licenses — Chinese hyperscalers accelerating domestic alternatives instead

Commerce Department official told Congress on July 14 that H200 shipments to China have been 'very few' since the January 2026 case-by-case regime opened, even though roughly $10B in export licenses have been approved. Combined with the 25% national-security tariff, 50%-of-US-volume cap, and end-use certification requirements, effective Chinese uptake has been modest. Chinese hyperscalers (Alibaba, Baidu, Huawei/CXMT ecosystem) are accelerating domestic accelerator and HBM development instead — CXMT DRAM share now ~8% in Q1 (from ~3% a year earlier).
