Anthropic signs $35B compute deal with Nvidia-backed Lambda anchored on Hut 8's 352MW Texas site — Bloomberg reports Anthropic has agreed to a $35B multi-year compute deal with Lambda, a cloud provider backed by Nvidia. The infrastructure sits at Hut 8's 352MW campus in Nueces County TX (Beacon Point), with a four-party chain: Hut 8 builds and leases the space to Nvidia, Nvidia leases capacity to Lambda, Lambda installs Nvidia GPUs and sells the resulting compute to Anthropic. Read-through: direct incremental GPU demand for NVDA on top of the existing hyperscaler capex cycle, and a second major merchant-power datacenter validation for Hut 8 following Nvidia's separate capacity booking a few weeks ago. Hut 8 opened +4% premarket; Benchmark analyst has a $195 PT (+143% upside).
