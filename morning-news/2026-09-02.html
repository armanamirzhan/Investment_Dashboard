Dell blows out Q2 FY27: revenue +58% to $47B, EPS $7.04 vs $4.92 est, AI backlog jumps to $95B from $51B; stock +9% after-hours — Dell reported Q2 FY27 after Tuesday's close: revenue $47.0B (+58% YoY, ~$2.16B above consensus), non-GAAP EPS $7.04 vs $4.92 est (+203% YoY). AI-Optimized Server revenue doubled YoY and orders drove the AI backlog to a record $95B, up from the $51.3B disclosed at Q1 - a $43.7B QoQ increase that is larger than the prior full-year revenue of the AI-server business. Read-through: strongly positive for GPU demand (NVDA, AMD) and datacenter-power/cooling adjacencies (VRT, ETN, APH); confirms hyperscaler-plus-neocloud capex cycle still accelerating.
