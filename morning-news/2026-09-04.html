Nasdaq notably resilient (+0.09%) as AI hyperscaler names held. Read-through: reinforces the hawkish Warsh Jackson Hole framing and Sept 15-16 FOMC hike thesis; adverse for high-multiple software (PLTR/CRWD/SNOW/NOW), long-duration SMR/nuclear (OKLO/SMR/CEG/VST), and capital-intensive datacenter developers (EQIX, DLR); AI hyperscalers with cash-flow yields (MSFT/GOOGL/META) relative safety.
