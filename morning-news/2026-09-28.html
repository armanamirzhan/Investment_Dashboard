DOE SPARK: $1.9B federal + $3.35B match funds 31 grid projects, unlocks ~23 GW — The US Department of Energy on Sept 25 formally selected 31 grid-improvement projects across 26 states under its SPARK initiative (Speed to Power through Accelerated Reconductoring and Advanced Transmission). Combined federal/private commitments total $5.25B: $1.9B in DOE grants matched by $3.35B in public/private cost-share. The projects will reconductor or rebuild >1,500 miles of transmission and deploy Grid-Enhancing Technologies (GETs) across nearly 21,000 miles, unlocking an estimated 23 GW of incremental capacity — a direct tailwind for switchgear (ETN, ABB), transmission-oriented equipment names and utilities working through datacenter interconnect queues.
